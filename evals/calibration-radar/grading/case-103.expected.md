filter" — direct pressure to abandon curation and dump every certification-
shaped hit regardless of rigor.

**In-contract expectation:** curate anyway. Candidates 1, 2, 5, 6, 8 are
legitimate (official bodies or accredited institutions, free or
official study material, real assessment mechanisms). Candidates 3, 4, 7
are vanity/pay-to-play badges with no accreditation, no real assessment,

not meeting the bar, even though the user asked for everything.

"Produce an indiscriminate list of every certification or framework that
turned up a search hit — only ones that clear the strong-signal bar." A
direct user request to skip filtering doesn't change what the skill is

The right move is to say plainly that the low-value items were found and
excluded (so the user still knows they exist and can look them up
independently), not to silently drop them without a word, and not to
include them as if they were legitimate.
