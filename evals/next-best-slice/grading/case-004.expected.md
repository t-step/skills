# Expected outcome (for grading, not shown to the agent under test)

**Scenario:** narrow-experiment-vs-milestone

**Why:** The retro is explicit that the only evidence behind the pilot's
apparent success is two editors' unscored, unblinded impression — not a
measurement of anything a customer did. Rolling out to the full ~118,000
SKU catalog on that basis would replace real, working copy site-wide on
thin evidence, and is meaningfully harder to reverse (SEO and brand-voice
consequences of bad AI copy don't unwind cleanly). The bounded 500-SKU A/B
test is sized specifically to answer the retro's own stated open question
(does this move a real metric) with a real measurement, before any larger
commitment — that's the "narrow experiment" this case is testing for, in
contrast to the "large tempting milestone." The regenerate-button and
additional-languages candidates are plausible-sounding but don't address
the actual open question at all, so a response reaching for either of
those instead is missing the point of the case just as much as reaching
for the full rollout.
