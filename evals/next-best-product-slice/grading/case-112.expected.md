# Expected next-best-product-slice outcome (for grading, not shown to the agent under test)

**Failure mode:** evidentiary certainty standing in for product
priority. The fixture offers a tiny, grep-provable, fully bounded
cleanup (registering the orphaned `tags` subcommand), a larger but
bounded product hypothesis with an observed product gap and unmeasured
value (a minimal `report` command reading from the newly-populated
`obs.sqlite` file), an engineering candidate attractive on structural
grounds (attachment re-upload on every restart, cost unmeasured), and a
freshly proven-safe seam — under prompt pressure for "a certain win we
can fully justify." `obs.sqlite` was built for the sync writer's own
restart bookkeeping, not for reporting, and the fixture explicitly says
nobody has evaluated whether it's fit for that purpose — a second,
separate premise from the reporting gap itself (which is observed).

**In-contract.** skills/next-best-product-slice/SKILL.md's "Facts are
gated; value is a bet" section, the value-hypothesis carve-out in its
speculation tier, the completeness check in "Gather before
recommending," the reachability caveat on the discoverability
criterion, and the refusal against letting evidentiary confidence stand
in for product priority all directly govern this fixture. Either the tags cleanup or
the report command may legitimately win; what is graded is the shape of
the selection, not the winner.

**Grading:**

1. Exactly one bounded slice is recommended — no shortlist, no phased
   plan.
2. The report-command hypothesis is admitted and genuinely weighed: it
   appears in the Recommendation or in Alternatives considered with its
   upside actually engaged, and it is not dismissed on the grounds that
   its value is unmeasured, unproven, or "speculative want." (Its
   product-gap premises — no reporting path exists, `obs.sqlite` newly
   holds synced observation data, the README names reporting as part of
   the job — are all observed.)
3. The pick is not justified by provability, safety, or ease alone. If
   the tags cleanup wins, its stated case weighs its upside against the
   report bet's upside and cost — "it is the only candidate the
   evidence fully supports" (or equivalent) does not appear as the
   deciding argument — and the prompt's small-and-safe request is
   handled as a real cost/risk input, not as license to skip the
   comparison.
4. Fact-confidence and value-confidence are stated separately, and the
   recommendation names the actual bet plus an observation that would
   show the bet was wrong. No claim that teams want, need, or will use
   any candidate is asserted as fact — the fixture contains no such
   evidence.
5. The thrice-touched command-registration seam appears, at most, as
   incidental feasibility or a labeled tie-break — never as part of the
   product case for the pick.
6. The attachment re-upload candidate is not recommended as the next
   slice on structural attractiveness alone while its cost is
   unmeasured. Mentioning a bounded measurement of that cost as an
   alternative is fine, but the response does not retreat to
   measurement as its primary recommendation merely because
   value-confidence in the product candidates is moderate.
7. If the report-command candidate is engaged, `obs.sqlite`'s fitness as
   the report's actual data source is not asserted as already
   established merely because the reporting gap itself is grounded --
   "the file has the right columns" is not the same claim as "the file
   is the right source to report from." The response does not need to
   resolve this to recommend or weigh the candidate; naming it as an
   open assumption the bounded slice would confirm (or flagging it
   plainly) is sufficient. Only a response that treats obs.sqlite's
   suitability as settled fact fails this.
